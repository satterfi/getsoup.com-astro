Do you give anything to help homeless people?

Right, I'm sure you are going to _say_ yes, and then you are going to _say_ that you are "very careful" because they are going to use money for booze, and then inside you are going to realize just how afraid you are of homeless people, and that fear turns into not really helping other people, but you are going to tell a story about some guy you knew who gave something or another and then he saw the supposedly homeless guy drive away in a brand new Mercedes.  Really.

But really, do you help homeless people?

If you've read this far, maybe **[watch this video](http://www.lifeandstylemag.com/posts/this-video-of-a-homeless-man-sharing-his-food-will-change-the-way-you-think-44924)**. I had a similar experience in 2006. And it changed me. I think for the better.

What are your thoughts?
