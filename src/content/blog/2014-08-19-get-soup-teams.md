We are all volunteers.  None of us are paid advocates -- we do this because we believe in the cause.   Any financial donations that do come towards Get Soup are used for to forward the cause of the local community kitchens.

**The Digital Media team**

The Digital Media team is responsible for the web presence.  This includes our reactive web site, the web logs, and writing our internet presence text.

**The Marketing team**

The Marketing team is responsible for getting the word out about community kitchens, and getting the word out about this service.  They attend homeless advocacy meetings, are involved with public and private schools, and other community involvement forums to help both community kitchen providers and the community connect in a meaningful way.

**The Development team**

The Development team is responsible for the front end user interface (phone, tablet, and browser based), for the "middle tier" engine that transforms a user's request for services in the area to a list of providers in that area, and for the database that houses the tens of thousands of providers in the world.
