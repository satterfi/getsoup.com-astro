Help us help you!  Your time, your ideas... we can use your help!

**Spread the word!**

The best way to help is to spread the word.  Mention Get Soup at

- your religious establishment
- your local unemployment office (those who have lost their jobs may need some assistance)
- your friends
- and even at your local soup kitchen!

**Add new services!**

Do you know of a soup kitchen or other social services group that is not yet listed in Get Soup?  Please add them to our database!  It is free for the provider, free for the user, and free for you.

**Have a new idea?**

Have you thought of a way to use Get Soup in a more creative way?  Please tell us!  We can use your help.

**Technical contribution**

Can you help with the technology?  Send us a note, we'd love you to be part of our team!
